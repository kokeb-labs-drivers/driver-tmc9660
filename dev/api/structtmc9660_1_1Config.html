◆ boot_timeout_ms

uint32_t tmc9660::Config::boot_timeout_ms {500}

Bound of every identity wait (start-up with hardware_reset, a start, Boot): each failed poll is charged 10 ms and, on UART, its reply wait, resync gap and drains (A9).
