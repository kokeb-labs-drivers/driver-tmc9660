Datasheet map¶

The driver follows the TMC9660 datasheet and the two reference manuals. This page says which revisions, where to get them, where each part of the driver is described in them, and, for every feature of the chip, whether the driver exposes it, guards it or leaves it out. Section names and page numbers are the documents' own (the printed page numbers).

  • Datasheet: TMC9660 70V Smart Gate Driver with Servo (FOC) Controller in HW and Buck Converter, Analog Devices (Trinamic) 19-101926, revision 1 (2024-11). Get it from the vendor page. SHA-256 of the file used: bfa29e7710b6b6a145aae98ff714342f393d8d8c4a7d14416b9e65202c07287d.

Coverage¶

Every feature of the chip is in one of three states:

  • Exposed: reachable through a typed call, with the checks of the generated tables (access, range, coded values). The driver does not decide what a value means for your motor.
  • Guarded: reachable, and the driver adds a rule so that the call cannot do harm by itself (Safety).
  • Out of scope: not in the driver, with the reason.

When the datasheet and the driver differ¶

The driver is written against the revisions above. If you have a newer revision, compare its revision history with the topics above; a change to a pin, a register, a timing or a value the driver uses is a bug report for this repository. Where the documents disagree among themselves, Ambiguities says what the driver does. Where the driver deliberately goes beyond or stays short of the documents, the Contract says so.

The repository never contains a PDF: vendors own their documents. Download them from the vendor, and compare their SHA-256 with the ones above to confirm you have the same files.
